# customer-journey.md

## Customer Journey Map for Code-Vault

### 1. Aware
- **Trigger Event**: Freelance developers encounter payment/IP disputes with clients.
- **Friction Points**: Lack of trust in payment processes; uncertainty about IP rights; previous negative experiences with disputes.
- **User Emotions**: Frustration, anxiety, skepticism.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Educational content on the importance of code escrow; testimonials from satisfied users; clear communication of benefits.
- **Metric**: Number of website visits and engagement with educational content.

### 2. Consider
- **Trigger Event**: Developers research solutions to mitigate payment/IP disputes.
- **Friction Points**: Overwhelming number of options; confusion about features and benefits; concerns about security and reliability.
- **User Emotions**: Cautious optimism, confusion, indecision.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Clear comparison charts; free resources (e.g., guides, webinars); personalized consultations to address specific needs.
- **Metric**: Number of demo requests and inquiries.

### 3. Try
- **Trigger Event**: Developers sign up for a free trial or demo of Code-Vault.
- **Friction Points**: Complexity of setup; unclear instructions; initial skepticism about effectiveness.
- **User Emotions**: Hopeful, but anxious about the learning curve.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Intuitive onboarding process; responsive customer support; interactive tutorials or walkthroughs.
- **Metric**: Trial activation rate and user engagement during the trial.

### 4. Adopt
- **Trigger Event**: Developers successfully complete their first transaction using Code-Vault.
- **Friction Points**: Potential technical issues; concerns about the reliability of the platform; need for ongoing support.
- **User Emotions**: Relief, satisfaction, cautious trust.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Follow-up support; success stories shared; community engagement (forums, user groups).
- **Metric**: Conversion rate from trial to paid subscription.

### 5. Expand
- **Trigger Event**: Developers experience the benefits of Code-Vault and consider using it for more projects.
- **Friction Points**: Price sensitivity; need for additional features; competition from other platforms.
- **User Emotions**: Confidence, loyalty, but also apprehension about costs.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Loyalty rewards; referral bonuses; regular updates on new features and improvements.
- **Metric**: Customer lifetime value (CLV) and upsell rate for additional features or services.
