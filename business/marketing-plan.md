## 6. Success Metrics (90‑day Targets)

| Metric | Target (Day +90) | Rationale |
|--------|------------------|-----------|
| **Daily Active Users (DAU)** | **≥ 500** active vaults (≥ 1 commit per day) | Indicates product stickiness; aligns with 100 % of paying customers using at least one vault per week. |
| **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** | **$30,000** | Assuming average revenue per user (ARPU) $30/mo → 1,000 paying seats (including free‑trial conversions). |
| **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Payback** | **≤ 3 months** | With blended CAC $233 and $30/mo ARPU, break‑even after ~8 months; aim to accelerate via upsell to $99/mo “Enterprise” tier. |
| **Conversion Rate (waitlist → paid)** | **5 %** (2,000 → 100) by D +30, **10 %** by D +90 | Early‑adopter enthusiasm + referral incentives. |
| **Churn (30‑day)** | **< 4 %** | Strong product‑market fit; escrow contracts are typically multi‑milestone, reducing early churn. |
| **Net Promoter Score (NPS)** | **≥ 55** (survey at D +60) | Target for a “must‑recommend” developer tool. |

*If any metric falls short by > 15 % at D +60, trigger a rapid‑iteration sprint: adjust pricing, boost referral incentives, or add a “dispute‑resolution” add‑on.*
