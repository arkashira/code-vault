# Business Model Canvas
## Value Proposition
* **Tamper-Evident Code Storage**: Securely store code and other intellectual property in a git-native vault, ensuring the integrity and authenticity of deliverables.
* **Milestone-Based Payment Release**: Automate payment release upon completion of predefined milestones, reducing the risk of non-payment and ensuring fair compensation for developers.
* **Cryptographically Timestamped Proof-of-Deliverable**: Generate immutable and verifiable proof of deliverables, providing a clear audit trail and protecting against disputes.

## Customer Segments
* **Software Development Teams**: Teams and individuals building software applications, seeking a secure and reliable way to store and manage their code.
* **Open-Source Communities**: Open-source projects and communities requiring a transparent and tamper-evident platform for collaborative development.
* **Enterprises**: Large organizations with complex software development workflows, needing a scalable and secure solution for intellectual property management.

## Channels
* **Web Application**: A user-friendly web interface for developers to create and manage their code vaults.
* **CLI Tool**: A command-line interface for power users and automation scripts.
* **Integration Partnerships**: Strategic partnerships with popular development platforms, such as GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket.
* **Content Marketing**: Blog posts, tutorials, and guides highlighting the benefits and best practices of using code-vault.

## Revenue Streams
* **Subscription Model**: Offer monthly or yearly subscription plans for individuals, teams, and enterprises, with tiered pricing based on storage capacity and feature requirements.
* **Transaction Fees**: Charge a small fee for each milestone payment release, providing an additional revenue stream.
* **Custom Solutions**: Offer bespoke solutions and support for large enterprises, generating revenue through professional services and licensing agreements.

## Cost Structure
* **Infrastructure Costs**: Expenses related to maintaining and scaling the code-vault platform, including server costs, storage, and bandwidth.
* **Development Team**: Salaries and benefits for the development team, responsible for maintaining and improving the platform.
* **Marketing and Advertising**: Expenses related to promoting the platform, including content creation, advertising, and event sponsorships.
* **Customer Support**: Costs associated with providing support and services to customers, including support staff, documentation, and training materials.

## Key Resources
* **Development Team**: A skilled team of developers, designers, and engineers responsible for building and maintaining the code-vault platform.
* **Infrastructure**: Scalable and secure infrastructure, including servers, storage, and networking equipment.
* **Partnerships**: Strategic partnerships with development platforms, industry leaders, and open-source communities.
* **Brand and Reputation**: A strong brand and reputation, built through delivering high-quality services and support.

## Key Activities
* **Platform Development**: Continuous development and improvement of the code-vault platform, including new feature development and bug fixing.
* **Marketing and Promotion**: Ongoing marketing and promotion efforts, including content creation, advertising, and event sponsorships.
* **Customer Support**: Providing high-quality support and services to customers, including documentation, training, and support staff.
* **Partnership Development**: Building and maintaining strategic partnerships with development platforms, industry leaders, and open-source communities.

## Key Partners
* **Development Platforms**: Partnerships with popular development platforms, such as GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket, to provide seamless integration and support.
* **Industry Leaders**: Collaborations with industry leaders and experts to stay up-to-date with the latest trends and best practices.
* **Open-Source Communities**: Partnerships with open-source communities to promote the use of code-vault and contribute to the development of the platform.
* **Security and Compliance**: Partnerships with security and compliance experts to ensure the code-vault platform meets the highest standards of security and compliance.
